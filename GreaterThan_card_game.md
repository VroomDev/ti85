====================================

       GREATER THAN (2-6P)

====================================

DECK: 52 cards + 2-3 Jokers (54 or 55 total).

DEAL: 7 cards (2-3P) | 5 cards (4-6P).

Flip 1 card to start discard pile.

(If Joker flips, 1st player sets it.)



PLAY (1 option per turn):

1. SINGLE (>): Same suit, strictly

   higher rank (2..10-J-Q-K-A-2).

   Modulo wrap: 2 beats Ace. No ties.

2. SET (22 > 9): 2, 3, or 4 of a kind

   on ANY card. Top card sets new

   suit & rank.

3. JOKER: Play on anything + 1 card

   to set new suit/rank (or play

   alone as final card to go out).



DRAW & ENDGAME:

- Draw until playable while deck has

  cards.

- Empty hand = drop out (1st, 2nd...);

  others keep playing.

- Empty deck = pass if blocked. Ends

  after 1 full round of passes.



RANKING (Remaining Players):

1. Fewest cards left wins.

2. Tie: Highest hand total wins

   (2-10=face, J/Q/K=10, A=11, Jkr=0).

3. Still tied: Highest single card

   wins (A > K > Q > J > 10 > ... > 2).
