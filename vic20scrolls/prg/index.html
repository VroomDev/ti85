VIC-20 Scrolls

Commodore Vic-20 versions of Scrolls for the TI-85 by Chris Busch (c) 1996

Scrolls was written for the TI-85 calculator by me 30 years ago! This port is a color and sound enhanced version. These .prg files are for the VIC-20 with 32K: one LVL pack (story, maps, and pictures) compiled with the Scrolls engine. Pick a pack in the list below. In VICE use xvic -memory all. Works on a real Vic-20 too!

Story

Because there can be many LVL packs, the story can change from one file to the next. The best-known story is this:

You need to retrieve the scroll of wisdom from a bunch of tree burnin' monsters who don't like you very much. At your disposal is your trusty sling shot.

Beware, danger awaits you. The monsters will try to punch you if they find you. And if that doesn't get you, the fire will burn you.

When you retrieve the Scroll of wisdom, you travel to another distant land and the quest continues.

Tips

  • You can only carry one key at a time. Use keys wisely.
  • Extra lives when your score hits 50, 150, 250, ... (max 9 lives).
  • Some walls are shootable and may reveal secret passages.
  • You can shoot trees, bombs, and walls to get rid of them.
  • The world is very fertile, that is why trees grow everywhere.

How to Play (keyboard and joystick)

Keys and joystick may be used together. Walk with I/J/L/M and fire with Shift (the VIC only reports one letter key at a time). Press fire or any key at the title to start; hold time seeds the random numbers.

Key / stickAction
I / joy upwalk up
J / joy leftwalk left
L / joy rightwalk right
M / joy downwalk down
Shift / fireshoot (facing stays; a short hold then you can walk while firing)
Qquit this run
Ppause; press P again to continue

After a scroll the next level begins.
