# Changelog

## 2026-11-11

- Map friends (P) wander, and you can bump them aside; they flinch ahead when pushed.
- Facing left or right picks which player picture is shown.
- Touching a tree may leave a coin, a bomb, or empty space.
- Bombs tumble with a facing, and seekers sometimes pick a random direction.
- Random rolls are whitened so short runs look less streaky.
- The default pack is Tomb (“Protect your friends!”), with updated player, bomb, and seeker pictures.

## 2026-11-04

- Backgrounds on some levels are corrected, and empty space is blue.
- Tomb is easier to play.
- A shootable brick copied from a solid brick shows one different pixel.

## 2026-11-02

- The random sequences the game actually uses are more evenly balanced.

## 2026-11-01

- Picking up a key removes it from the map.
- Help appears only before a game starts, and the help picture animates.

## 2026-10-28

- The status line stays steady.
- Help text is easier to follow.

## 2026-09-26

- After a long run the game switches to turbo speed.

## 2026-09-24

- Warp moved from S to W, and the title screen says so.
- Rare levels show the right graphics.

## 2026-09-22

- Pressing S warps to another level.

## 2026-09-20

- Water and lava stay in place when something would crush them.
- Boulders can be pushed, used as cover, or used to crush monsters.
- Shots leave in the direction you are facing.

## 2026-09-19

- The VIC-20 Scrolls games are playable.
- The overhead map is square.
- A monster can turn a tree into a bomb. Picking up a tree awards a coin.
